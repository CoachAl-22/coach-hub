Coach rules

  • Keep it short. 5 minutes for Foundation and 8 for Emerging. If athletes are restless, go straight to the drill.
  • Use it immediately. Apply every mind skill in the next running rep.
  • No comparing kids. Focus on each athlete’s own improvement and never rank children in this segment.
  • Normalise, don’t fix. Say “that’s your body getting ready” and offer the breath.
  • Flag, don’t counsel. Tell Alistair about ongoing anxiety or distress beyond normal race nerves.
  • Parent loop. Include the week’s skill in the parent recap so athletes can demonstrate it at home.

Kit

  • Cue cards for Emerging weeks 3, 7 and 8
  • Personal-best board or clipboard
  • Stopwatch or timing gates for weeks 6 and 10

Quick scripts

  • Box breath: “In for 4… hold for 4… out for 4… hold for 4.”
  • Balloon breath: “Hands on tummy. Blow up the balloon slowly… and let it go.”
  • Nerves: “Butterflies mean your body is getting ready to go fast.”
